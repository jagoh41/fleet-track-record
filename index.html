Before you read the numbers

  • This is days of data, not years. Nothing here is statistically meaningful yet.
  • The account is small — £2,000 at the open, and it had never traded before this record began. Percentages on an account this size swing hard and do not transfer to a larger one. Read the £ column.
  • Money paid in or taken out is not performance. Every transfer is listed in the summary and subtracted from the return figures, so a withdrawal never shows up as a loss. The balance chart still steps at the moment it happens.
  • Anything left of the publication marker was already known when this page went up, and is shaded. If nothing is shaded, nothing was.
  • Open positions are excluded from realised P&L and can still reverse.
